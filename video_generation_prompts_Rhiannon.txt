TITLE: Rhiannon - The Strength to Endure

In ancient Wales, there lived a woman named Rhiannon.

She was admired for her grace, wisdom, and strength.

But her greatest test was yet to come.

One night, her newborn son disappeared.

Without proof.

Without witnesses.

And without mercy.

The people blamed her.

They accused her of a crime she never committed.

Though innocent, Rhiannon accepted a punishment no one should bear.

Year after year, she endured humiliation, judgment, and isolation.

Not because she was weak.

But because she knew the truth.

And she refused to let bitterness consume her.

Then, after years of suffering, the missing child was found.

Alive.

The truth emerged.

And every accusation collapsed.

Rhiannon's name was cleared.

But her true victory wasn't proving others wrong.

It was remaining strong when the entire world doubted her.

Because sometimes courage isn't fighting back.

Sometimes courage is holding on.

Holding on until the truth has a chance to speak.

Rhiannon's story reminds us: resilience and honour can outlast any lie.

Final screen text:

"Truth may be delayed, but it is never defeated."
